# ACE Exchange V6 — Visual Identity

## Approved core palette
- Midnight navy: `#07111F`
- Deep navy: `#0A1B35`
- Surface blue: `#102746`
- Dark surface: `#0C1728`
- Electric blue: `#2563EB`
- Bright fintech blue: `#3B82F6`
- Cyan accent: `#22D3EE`
- Soft blue-white: `#EAF2FF`
- White: `#FFFFFF`
- Muted text: `#94A3B8`

## Brand rule
ACE Exchange is not ACE Trading Frameworks. Do not use black/gold luxury styling, serif-led typography, dealer-desk terminology, or ACE Trading Frameworks visual motifs as the dominant Exchange identity.

## Product character
Premium global fintech · institutional-grade exchange UI · clear · secure-feeling · data-led · contemporary · international · restrained.

## UI rules
- Navy surfaces are dominant; electric blue is the primary action color.
- Cyan is a micro-accent for intelligence, connectivity and focus states.
- Green/red are reserved for actual positive/negative states and market direction.
- Avoid excessive glow, gradients, crypto-neon or casino visual language.
- All interactive controls require visible keyboard focus.
- Reduced-motion preference is respected globally.
